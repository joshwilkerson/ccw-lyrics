# Only Jesus

### Verse 1

Come and behold the Lamb of God
Bearing our sins upon the cross
Purchasing freedom by His blood
Worthy is the Lamb

### Verse 2

Come and adore the risen Son
Highly exalted far above
Give Him all glory, praise and love
Worthy is the Lamb

### Chorus

No one else no other name
In heaven or earth can save
Only Jesus, Only Jesus
(repeat)

### Verse 3

Let every creature that has breath
Bow down before Him and confess
Christ is the Lord, He’s conquered death
Worthy is the Lamb

### Bridge

You’re just too good
Too good to be forgotten
You’re just too good
Too good to not be fknown
And I will give my life
To see You lifted high
Worthy is the Lamb
